Keeping Your Broken Arrow HVAC System Running Longer

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, central air usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and causes far less stress than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of summer.

Minor issues rarely stay small for long. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a few miserable nights.
